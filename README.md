StockFirst

A Stock market learning simulator for beginners that helps build real habits
-Work in progress | Not financial advice.

This is a stock market trading simulator that features three sections: a tutorial, a quest system, and a trading simulator. Starting with the tutorial, users will learn how to use the platform and important terms within the trading industry. Additionally, this will provide beginners with an understanding of what is happening within the trading market, helping them avoid feeling overwhelmed. Shifting over to the Quest system helps reinforce positive traits and ensures traders don't develop bad habits. In addition, it helps guide users on their journey and informs them when they are ready to move on to more advanced resources and learn from them. Finally, the trading simulator will be where users perform their trading within the platform, completing quests and engaging in hands-on learning.

I wanted to make this small interactive model in order to help promote financial literacy. Furthermore, I have also noticed that other simulators use exaggerated or inaccurate data, which prevents users from building a realistic understanding of risk. I want users to come away from this not as fully ready traders, but as people understanding there is still much to learn with trading, and to be careful.
